# Assumptions and Unknowns

## Assumptions

- Robotics teams can provide 3-5 real STL/STEP parts for testing.
- A simple local supplier directory is enough for a demo.
- DFM warnings are valuable even when heuristic.
- Iteration-speed metrics matter for the YC story.

## Unknowns

- Which DFM rules catch the most real robot-part issues.
- Whether teams prefer FDM, CNC, or laser cutting for their urgent parts.
- Actual local supplier response times.
- Whether users will pay before a real supplier network exists.
